Research

Representative papers are shown below.

Reeling It In: Flexible Needle Pick Up via Thread Manipulation for Autonomous Suturing
Emma Huang, Zih-Yun Chiu, Neelay Joglekar, Shanglei Liu, Michael C. Yip
IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), 2026
[arXiv] [paper] [presentation] [details]

Image to robot motion pipeline written in Python and C++ on the da Vinci Research Kit (dVRK): SAM segmentation → point cloud reconstruction (RAFT-stereo) → 3D B-Spline fitting → Open3D integration → intersection/reliability calculations → dependency aware grasping goal selection → robot motion planning. 61% full pipeline success, 91% reconstruction success. Accepted to IROS 2026.

Integrated Respiratory Sensing and Gated Control for Autonomous Needle Insertion Via CT Bore Robotic Arm
Emma Huang*, Moira Bohley*, Krishna Jamakhandi**, Rebecca Fellbaum**, Sophia Tang, Sean Tutton, Albert Hsiao, Alexander Norbash, Changwei Chen, Derek Chen, Ananya Rao, Lizzie Peiros, Michael C. Yip (* / ** denote equal contribution)
Under review for ICRA 2027, 2026
[arXiv] [paper] [details]

Designed and built a torso phantom and needle insertion testbed with integrated respiratory sensing and gated control for CT-guided robotic procedures. Led a team of 12 undergraduates, Masters, and PhD students, resulting in a 29.8% reduction in breath tracking error and successful needle insertions across all trials. Under review for ICRA 2027.

Live Thread Reconstruction for Autonomous Manipulation
Emma Huang, Omar Hernandez, Zih-Yun Chiu, Michael Yip
IROS DOM-R3 Workshop, 2026
[arXiv] [paper] [details]

Presents live suture thread reconstruction methods for autonomous manipulation tasks in surgical robotics, providing real-time visual tracking and spatial representations essential for dynamic needle pick-up and thread untangling.

Real-time Thread Reconstruction for Autonomous Suture Thread and Needle Manipulation
Emma Huang, Omar Hernandez, Zakai Liang, Zih-Yun Chiu, Michael Yip
Under review for IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), 2026
[details]

Autonomous thread reconstruction improving on prior work with autonomous segmentation, keypoint ordering, and real-time tracking to drive manipulation strategies for needle pick up, thread untangling, and tightening. Demonstrated a 25.8% improvement in needle pick up trials and 88.9% average task completion success. Under review for RAL journal.
