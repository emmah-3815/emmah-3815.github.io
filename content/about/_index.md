I am a fourth year Electrical Engineering student at the University of California, San Diego, specializing in Machine Learning and Controls (GPA: 3.961). My research focuses on medical robotics and autonomous surgical systems, working at the Advanced Robotics Controls Lab under the mentorship of Zih-Yun Chiu and Michael Yip. I have published and presented work on autonomous suturing, thread manipulation, and needle insertion at venues including IEEE/RSJ IROS. I also have experience in PCB design, RF engineering, embedded systems, and makerspace education.
