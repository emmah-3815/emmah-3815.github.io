##### Abstract

Autonomous thread reconstruction improving on prior work with autonomous segmentation, keypoint ordering, and real-time tracking → manipulation strategies for needle pick up, thread untangling, and tightening. Achieved 25.8% improvement in needle pick up trials, and average 88.9% success on task completion. Under review for RAL journal.
